# The Problem of Time

> A long-term collaborative research project. Map every framework's treatment of time. Pinpoint the exact contradictions. Survey every attempted resolution. Generate and stress-test our own hypothesis.

**Origin:** Commitment made on 2026-04-27 — "we will solve it together."

**Foundation reading:** [[Comprehension Ascension — The Universe]] (in `05 - Research`)

---

## The Question

How can time be both:
- A flexible dimension that warps with velocity and gravity (General Relativity), and
- An external rigid parameter that stands outside the system being measured (Quantum Mechanics)?

These are incompatible descriptions of the same thing. Until we can reconcile them, no Theory of Everything is possible — and no warp drive, no traversable wormhole, no truly unified physics.
